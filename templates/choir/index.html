YOUR FIRST REHEARSAL

A melody is lovely.
Harmony is a conversation.

These guide tones play the written notes so you can sing along. No microphone or recording needed.

  1. Meet your part. Choose a song and your voice. Tap “Hear my part” and listen before you sing.
  2. Take your time. Slow the music down. The blue line follows the sound, and the notes light up as they play.
  3. Join the others. Tap “Sing with the others” to silence your guide part. Can you hold your melody while the other voices sing?

Soprano and alto are the higher lines; tenor and bass are lower. Choose a comfortable part, sing gently, and ask your teacher if the notes feel too high or low. You can sing a lower part an octave higher.

What makes a piece important?

This is a curated selection of 100 influential works and 50 composers in the Western classical tradition: music that changed a form, opened a new sound world, influenced later musicians, or became central to performance and listening. The numbers mark chronological order, not a ranking.

These pages follow one musical tradition. They do not tell the whole history of music, and even this canon reflects unequal access to education, publication, and performance. Rediscovery keeps changing the story.

A note on dates & sources

Dates usually identify composition or completion; publication and premiere dates are labeled. “c.” means approximately. Era boundaries overlap and are useful signposts rather than fixed rules. A collection, opera cycle, or complete set counts as one selection.
